Privacy Policy for Bubble Shooter

Developer: Tapan Kumar Mahanta

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tapan Kumar Mahanta ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us") handles user information when you use the Bubble Shooter Android mobile game ("the App").

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

  • Optional Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication): If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, we receive basic authentication information provided by Google (such as your Firebase User ID, display name, and email address) solely to identify your player account and restore your progress when you reinstall the App or switch devices.
  • Cloud Game Progress (Firebase Cloud Firestore): When signed in, your Game ID, display nickname, unlocked level, coin balance, level stars, high scores, power-up inventory, daily reward streak, mission status, and lifetime bubble statistics are securely stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Guest Mode (Local Storage): If you play as a Guest without signing in, your progress and audio/vibration preferences are stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the App in Guest mode will erase local data.
  • Network Connectivity Status: The App checks whether your device has an active Mobile Data or Wi-Fi connection before serving rewarded ads and granting ad-based rewards.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display Rewarded Ads and Rewarded Interstitial Ads. AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), approximate IP-based location, app interaction data, and diagnostic information to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on trusted Google services that operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your cloud progress remains stored so you can resume your exact level after reinstalling the game. You may sign out at any time from the in-game Profile menu or reset your progress in Settings.

To request permanent deletion of your Firebase account and all cloud-saved game data, please send an email with your Game ID or registered Google email address to Tapan Kumar Mahanta at bapunmaxx@gmail.com. We will process verified deletion requests within 7 business days.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

6. Changes to This Policy

Developer Tapan Kumar Mahanta may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as new features are added. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
