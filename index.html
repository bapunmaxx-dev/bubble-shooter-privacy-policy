Privacy Policy for Bubble Shooter

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bubble Shooter ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile game application on Android.

1. Information We Collect and Store Locally

Bubble Shooter lets you play immediately as a guest without creating an account or signing in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location directly.

The following game data is stored locally on your device only so your progress is saved:

  • Guest profile identifier and customizable display nickname
  • Unlocked levels, star ratings, high scores, and gameplay statistics
  • In-game coins, power-up inventory, daily rewards, and sound/haptic preferences

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display Rewarded Ads and Rewarded Interstitial Ads. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve ad performance, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AD_ID)
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)
  • Device model, OS version, app interactions, and ad performance diagnostics

Learn more about how Google handles data in advertising: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use our partners' apps.

3. Permissions Used

  • Internet & Network State: Required to load and display advertisements.
  • Vibrate: Used only for optional gameplay haptic feedback (can be disabled in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob for ad delivery and measurement.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, you can delete it anytime by:

  • Using the Reset Progress option inside the App's Settings menu, or
  • Clearing App Storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the App.

You can also reset or limit your Android Advertising ID anytime in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

5. Children's Privacy

Bubble Shooter does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently provided, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
